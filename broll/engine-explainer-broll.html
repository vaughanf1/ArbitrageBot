Buy every outcome. One of them has to pay $1.

04Firing the basket · 1 Aug

Four legs go out. They don't come back even.

A basket is only hedged up to its thinnest leg. Everything above that line is a naked bet — so it gets sold straight back.

05Why most "free money" isn't

The exchange takes a bite out of every leg.

Commission runs about 0.06 × price × (1 − price) per contract, per leg. On a four-leg basket that is charged four times — which is how a 2% edge becomes a loss.
